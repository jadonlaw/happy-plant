Set up in three steps.

  1. Clip it on

    Push the probe into the soil and clip the case onto the pot.

  2. Connect it

    Join it to your home Wi-Fi and pair it with the code that comes in your email.

  3. Check the face

    Open the app. Happy means leave it alone. Meh or sad tells you exactly why.
